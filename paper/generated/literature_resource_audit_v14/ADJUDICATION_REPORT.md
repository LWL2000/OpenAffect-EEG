# Model-assisted post-lock adjudication of the v14 literature-resource audit

This is a **model-assisted post hoc adjudication** of two independently locked AI coding outputs. It is not a human adjudication, and the two AI coders do not satisfy the coding manual's requirement for two independent human coders. No paper text, original coding CSV, or lock file was modified.

## Source integrity and comparison scope

The comparison script verified both locks against the current source CSV hashes before producing the pre-adjudication diagnostics in this directory. Coder A locked 24 paper rows and 60 evaluation rows; coder B locked 24 paper rows and 57 evaluation rows. Both coders agreed that all 24 access-conditioned papers lacked an eligible learned non-EEG comparator. That descriptive result remains conditioned on publicly validated full-text access and is not an unbiased field-prevalence estimate.

The locked paper files contained five disagreements: one `claim_type` and four `confidence` values. Four papers also had different evaluation-unit counts. `paper_adjudication.csv` records the paper-level decisions, and `protocol_unit_crosswalk.csv` records every unit mapping.

## Paper-level adjudication

| Paper | Field | A | B | Post-lock decision | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P034 | `claim_type` | `eeg_prediction_only` | `method_comparison` | `eeg_prediction_only` | The evaluated models predict affect from EEG. Chance expectations and clean/noisy or device/data conditions do not constitute an EEG architecture/feature-only comparison under the manual's `method_comparison` definition. |
| P014 | `confidence` | `high` | `medium` | `medium` | Main ten-fold fold construction and trial grouping are not stated, even though the separate LOSOCV is explicit. |
| P028 | `confidence` | `high` | `medium` | `medium` | Five-fold CV is reported without the sampling/grouping unit. |
| X2302 | `confidence` | `low` | `medium` | `medium` | The paper-level classifications and 80/20 five-fold design are directly stated; missing fold grouping reduces confidence but does not make the core classification weakly supported. |
| X2503 | `confidence` | `high` | `medium` | `high` | Whole-trial LOSO, source/held-out participant roles, and channel voting are explicit. Unit granularity remains a definitional issue rather than an evidence-access gap. |

## Protocol-unit adjudication

| Paper | Locked counts A/B | Post-lock handling | Evidence-based conclusion |
|---|---:|---|---|
| P011 | 2 / 1 | 2 units | SEED-IV is explicitly evaluated and has reported four-class results; coder B omitted this dataset unit. |
| P014 | 4 / 2 | 4 units | Both DEAP and DREAMER have a main ten-fold evaluation and a separately reported LOSOCV evaluation. |
| P032 | 1 / 2 | 2 units | Valence/arousal use 6 s windows, whereas stress/calm uses 2 s windows and task-specific tuning; these are materially different prediction units. |
| X2503 | 3 / 2 | Preserve A=3 and B=2 | DEAP binary and four-class tasks use different targets and LDA dimensions but the same whole-trial LOSO and label-resource access. The manual does not supply an objective threshold that makes either the split or combined representation uniquely correct. |

For X2503, the crosswalk therefore retains coder A's separate binary/four-class DEAP rows and coder B's combined DEAP row. No synthetic consensus row or single adjudicated unit count was created. Evaluation-level kappa remains withheld because a unique common unitization was not imposed; the crosswalk supports transparent row-level comparison without manufacturing agreement.

## Interpretation

The adjudication resolves factual omissions and protocol distinctions that are explicit in the full text while preserving the only irreducible granularity choice. The `summary.json` and `REPORT.md` generated by `summarize_dual_ai_literature_audit_v14.py` are pre-adjudication diagnostics; their `protocol_alignment_pending` language describes the state before the crosswalk in this report was added. Paper-level agreement statistics remain based on the locked pre-adjudication files, as required by the manual.
