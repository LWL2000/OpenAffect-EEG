# Socio-affective emotion regulation (ds006866)

## Scope

This external validation dataset contains 148 participants and 35,507 image
presentations across six emotion-regulation conditions. Of 35,506 readable EEG
trials, 35,504 have complete valence and arousal ratings.

## Benchmark use

- Source: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds006866
- Snapshot: 1.0.0
- Context: affective image viewing and regulation
- Supported audits: strict subject holdout, directional social/nonsocial context
  transfer, joint participant-context transfer, and condition identity
- Blocked audits: stimulus and participant-stimulus joint holdout, because
  public events do not expose trial-level image identity

All 148 source recordings share the same 64-channel EEG set. Two recordings
also contain one EMG channel; EMG and the declared ocular, cardiac, skin-
conductance, and trigger channels are excluded from the EEG input contract.

The dataset is an external validation domain, not a core training domain. The
repository does not redistribute EEGLAB recordings or image stimuli.

## External-audit result boundary

Across five fixed seeds, a semantic-prior-plus-pretrained-LaBraM residual model
reaches macro CCC 0.551 and 0.549 in the two directional context transfers when
participants repeat across fit and test. Participant identity is nearly
perfectly linearly decodable in those settings. When participant and context
are held out together, pretrained EEG-only CCC falls to 0.032 and 0.029, and
the hybrid does not show a stable advantage over the semantic prior or the
fixed-random LaBraM control. These results support a personalization-versus-
transfer distinction; the identity probes are representation audits, not
causal evidence that the affect predictor uses identity.
