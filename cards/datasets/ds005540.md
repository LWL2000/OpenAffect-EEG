# EmoEEG-MC (ds005540)

## Scope

EmoEEG-MC includes video-viewing and guided-imagery EEG. The public snapshot
contains 60 participants; the 30 participants with aligned self-report labels
contribute 1,260 benchmark trials across the two matched contexts.

## Benchmark use

- Source: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds005540
- Contexts: video viewing and text-guided imagery
- Identity fields: participant, eliciting item, and context
- Supported audits: subject, stimulus, joint subject-stimulus, directional
  context transfer, and core dataset holdout
- Foundation-model audit: official CBraMod and LaBraM pretrained weights versus
  fixed-seed same-architecture random initialization

Published reorder arrays are 64 channels by 6,000 samples at 200 Hz and are
used in microvolts. EEG arrays, derivatives, and movie files are not included
in the public benchmark package.
