# MusicEEG (ds002721)

## Scope

MusicEEG contains EEG recorded while 31 participants listened to film-music
excerpts and reported experienced affect. OpenAffect-EEG models trial-level
valence and arousal and preserves soundtrack identity for leakage audits.

## Benchmark use

- Source: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds002721
- Context: music listening
- Identity fields: participant and soundtrack
- Supported audits: random trial, subject holdout, stimulus holdout, joint
  subject-stimulus holdout, and core dataset holdout
- Not applicable: directional video-to-imagery transfer

The benchmark distributes harmonized metadata, split definitions, and derived
evaluation outputs only. Users obtain EEG and soundtrack resources from their
original sources under the applicable terms.
