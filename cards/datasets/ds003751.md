# DENS (ds003751)

## Scope

DENS contains naturalistic-video EEG from 40 participants with dimensional and
categorical affect ratings. OpenAffect-EEG joins behavior by stimulus identity,
not row position, and records trials whose public signal files are unreadable.

## Benchmark use

- Source: https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds003751
- Context: naturalistic video viewing
- Identity fields: participant and video
- Supported audits: random trial, subject holdout, stimulus holdout, joint
  subject-stimulus holdout, and core dataset holdout
- Known limitation: public `.fdt` lengths disagree with some `.set` headers;
  skipped trials remain explicit in feature metadata

No raw EEG or video stimulus is redistributed by this repository.
